# Example images

The example photos on the landing page. All are in the public domain. They were downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons on 2026-09-24, converted to sRGB, resized with Lanczos to at most 1600 px on the
long side, and saved as progressive JPEG (quality 84). Each has a `-thumb.jpg` 240 px on the short side.

| File | Title | Author | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mona-lisa.jpg` | Mona Lisa (c. 1503–1506) | Leonardo da Vinci; photo C2RMF, cropped and relevelled by Wikimedia Commons users | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mona_Lisa,_by_Leonardo_da_Vinci,_from_C2RMF_retouched.jpg | Public domain |
| `astronaut.jpg` | Christina M. Hammock (Koch), official NASA portrait, 10 January 2014 | NASA / Robert Markowitz | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Christina_M._Hammock_portrait.jpg (from https://www.flickr.com/photos/nasa2explore/12774511004/) | Public domain (work of NASA) |
| `mandarin-duck.jpg` | Mandarin duck (male), January 2005 | Adrian Pingstone | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mandarin.duck.arp.jpg | Public domain (released by the author) |

`hero-mosaic.webp` is made from `astronaut.jpg` with PhotoBrick itself: the XL mosaic (5 × 6
panels, Natural style, default settings) exported as PNG, with the left 40 % replaced by the same
crop of the photo. WebP quality 74, 600 × 720 px.
